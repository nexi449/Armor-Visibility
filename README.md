# Hide your armor without losing protection.

ArmorVisibility lets you hide the visual appearance of armor while keeping all protection and enchantments active.

Hide your own armor or the armor of other players and enjoy your skins without sacrificing protection.

---

## Download the mod

The mod is available to download on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/armorvisibility), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/armorvisibility) and [GitHub](https://github.com/nexi449/Armor-Visibility).

---

## Features

ArmorVisibility gives you control over which armor pieces are visible.

- Hide individual armor pieces.
- Hide all armor at once.
- Control the visibility of your own armor.
- Control the visibility of other players' armor.
- Toggle helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots individually.
- Keep armor protection and enchantments active.

---

## Compatibility

ArmorVisibility supports Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge.
